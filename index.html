About

I'm a man in his midlife, turning the pages of my diary and sharing them with the world through songs. My music is a reflection of life through my eyes — the emotions, relationships, people, and moments that make us who we are. Rooted in a sense of belonging and the connections we share, these songs are little pieces of my life, put to melody. A life, a diary, a few songs. Maybe a story that feels like yours, too. — Mitul Surti
