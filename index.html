The story behind the name

A diary, opened to the world.

I'm a man in midlife, turning the pages of my diary and sharing the songs I find there.

My music is a reflection of life through my eyes — the emotions, relationships, people and moments that make us who we are. Rooted in belonging and the connections we share, these songs are little pieces of life, put to melody.

A life, a diary, a few songs. Maybe a story that feels like yours, too.
